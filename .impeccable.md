## Design Context

### Users
KTH AI Society serves students exploring artificial intelligence and industry partners engaging with that community. The homepage should help visitors quickly understand the society, discover events and projects, and feel confident that the organization is active and credible.

### Brand Personality
Polished, restrained, and forward-looking. The interface should feel credible for sponsors and inviting for students without drifting into flashy startup aesthetics.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing clean blue-and-white identity, then reinforce it with subtle motion and quiet technical cues. Favor light mode, mobile-friendly layouts, and motion that feels deliberate rather than playful.

### Design Principles
- Use motion to guide attention, not to decorate.
- Keep interactions calm, fast, and readable on mobile.
- Preserve trust with refined, sponsor-friendly presentation.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences by default.
- Let the homepage feel active through choreography, not visual noise.
